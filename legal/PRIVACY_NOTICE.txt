1.  Information that stays on your device
The Software stores the following locally, on your own computer, and does not transmit it to us or to anyone else:
·  your saved designs, vault and portfolio files, and project files;
·  images You import, and images and build plans You export;
·  your application preferences and settings; and
·  your license key, stored so that You do not have to re-enter it.
Because these files live on your device, You are responsible for backing them up. If You uninstall the Software, remove your user data folder to delete them.
2.  License activation and validation
When You enter your license key, and periodically afterwards, the Software contacts Polar, the platform that issued your key, to confirm the license is valid and to record which devices are using it. The following is sent:

Why
Your license key
To identify the license being validated.
A product or organization identifier for the Software
So the licensing platform knows which product the key belongs to.
The version of the Software You are running
So we can support the version You actually have.

The Software does not send your name, email address, computer name, user name, file names, file contents, hardware inventory or location as part of license validation.
If the licensing service cannot be reached, the Software keeps working for a grace period rather than locking You out. Your license does not expire, and if we ever stop operating license validation we will publish a build or method that lets a licensed copy keep running without it.
3.  Update checks
The Software can check whether a newer version has been released. It does this by downloading a small public file that lists the current version number and a link to the download page. That file is hosted on GitHub.
The check is a request for a public file. The Software does not send your license key, your device label, an activation identifier or any account information with it. As with any internet request, the host receives your IP address and the standard request metadata that your operating system and network send. The comparison between the published version and your installed version is performed on your own computer.
If You would rather the Software did not check for updates, You can turn the check off in the application settings and check the product page manually instead.

5.  Why we handle this information
We handle the information described above in order to confirm that a license is valid, to apply the device limit that comes with a license, to tell You when an update is available, to prevent abuse of license keys, and to provide support when You ask for it. We do not use it for any other purpose.
If You are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with You, for license validation and device management; and our legitimate interests in preventing license abuse, in informing You of updates and in maintaining the security of the Software, for the remainder. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have considered the limited nature of the data and the fact that no profiling, advertising or tracking is involved.
6.  How long it is kept
License and activation records are kept for as long as the license exists, and for a reasonable period afterwards so that we can resolve support, refund or abuse questions. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with the matter and for our own records afterwards. Server logs held by our providers are kept for the short periods set out in their own policies.
We do not maintain analytics records, usage histories or profiles, because the Software does not produce them.
7.  What the Software does not do
·  It does not send your designs, portfolio files, exported images or imported images anywhere.

·  It does not include advertising, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers.
·  It does not run behavioral analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
·  It does not read files outside the folders You open or save to.
·  It does not fingerprint your hardware.
·  It does not use your personal data or your designs to train large language models or other artificial intelligence models, and we do not sell or license your data to anyone who does.
If any of this changes in a future version, we will update this notice, describe the change in the release notes, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent before the change takes effect for You.
