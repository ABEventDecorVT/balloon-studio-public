THEY ARE PROVIDED FOR PLANNING PURPOSES AND MAY BE INACCURATE, INCOMPLETE, OUTDATED OR UNSUITABLE FOR YOUR PARTICULAR APPLICATION. YOU MUST INDEPENDENTLY VERIFY EVERY VALUE BEFORE RELYING ON IT.
The following examples are illustrative rather than exhaustive:
(a)  Arch and column structures. Reported widths, heights, spans, cluster and row counts, spacing, curvature, tangential layout refits, painted balloon sizes and overall dimensions are mathematical and visual approximations based on simplified models, default assumptions, screen scaling and your inputs. Built structures may differ because of manufacturer tolerances, inflation variance, temperature, humidity, altitude, handling, tying method, frame hardware and installation technique.
(b)  Helium and lift calculations. Helium volume, lift capacity, weight required, bouquet counts, base-cluster settings and related figures are derived from reference charts, rounded values, assumed inflation sizes and simplified formulas. They do not account for every real-world variable, including under- or over-inflation, balloon quality, altitude, temperature, ribbon weight, attachments, treatment products, condensation, leakage, elapsed time since inflation, non-standard balloon types and mixed materials. A small difference in weight or gas volume may affect float time or safety margin.
(c)  Wall, link wall and panel layouts. Estimated widths, heights, balloon counts, duplet and link configurations and on-canvas scaling are planning aids and may not reflect finished installed dimensions.
(d)  Build plans and blueprints. Exported build plans, numbered cluster diagrams, portrait and landscape layouts and column blueprints are instructional references for experienced installers. They are not engineered drawings, stamped plans or site-specific specifications. Balloon identity numbers, pairings and layouts may not match field conditions after inflation or handling.
(e)  Scene builder, mockups and exports. Composited scenes, background images, exported images and on-screen presentations are for sales and design visualization only. They do not represent installed appearance, lighting, color accuracy, scale at the venue or structural feasibility.
(f)  Organic designs, add-in designer and user imports. Freehand layouts, add-in elements, imported images, vinyl and foil treatments and custom graphics are user-directed creative tools. Licensor does not warrant print quality, color fidelity, trademark compliance, copyright clearance or fitness of content You supply.
(g)  Color palettes and brand references. On-screen colors, including palette references to manufacturer color names, are approximations of physical balloon colors and vary with monitor calibration, lighting, production batch and manufacturer changes.
(h)  Availability of balloons, colors, sizes and foils. References to particular colors, finishes, sizes, brands, foil shapes or other materials are provided on a best-effort basis for planning. Licensor does not represent that any color is available in every size, format or product line, that a manufacturer will continue to offer a referenced item, or that any depicted combination is commercially available. Certain foil visuals, mockups, shapes or styles may be artist-created or generalized and may not correspond to an exact real-world product or vendor listing. You are responsible for verifying product availability, specifications, substitutions and sourcing before quoting, ordering, selling or building any design.
(i)  Saved designs and portfolio files. Files saved to vault, portfolio or project files may not reload identically across versions of the Software, operating systems or hardware, and should be reviewed before client delivery or installation.
